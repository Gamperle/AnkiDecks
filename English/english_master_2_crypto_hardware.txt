Vertrauenswürdige Ausführungsumgebung	TEE (Trusted Execution Environment)

Interaktiver Beweis	interactive proof

Sichere Mehrparteienberechnung	MPC (Secure Multi-Party Computation) — parties jointly compute a function without revealing their private inputs

Geheimnisteilung / Secret Sharing	secret sharing (e.g., Shamir's)

Differenzielle Privatheit	differential privacy — adds calibrated noise so individual records cannot be identified in aggregate results
Post-Quanten-Kryptographie	post-quantum cryptography
Gitterbasierte Kryptographie	lattice-based cryptography
Schlüsselkapselungsmechanismus	KEM (Key Encapsulation Mechanism)
Authentifiziertes Schlüsselaustauschprotokoll	authenticated key exchange (AKE)
TLS-Handshake (im Detail)	TLS handshake (ClientHello, ServerHello, key exchange, Finished)
Vorwärtsgeheimhaltung	forward secrecy / perfect forward secrecy (PFS)
Sitzungswiederaufnahme	session resumption (TLS)
0-RTT (Null-Roundtrip-Time)	0-RTT (Zero Round-Trip Time)
Zufallszahlengenerator	RNG (Random Number Generator)

Entropie	entropy — measure of randomness or unpredictability in data or a cryptographic key
Entropiequelle	entropy source
